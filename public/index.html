NAMI

Letters to tomorrow.

Write a letter to your future self, seal it in a bottle, and let the sea carry it to the day you choose. When that day arrives, it will be waiting on the shore.

Write a letter Wander into the village

波 (nami) means “wave” in Japanese.

The Village

Somewhere between yesterday and tomorrow there is a quiet seaside village. Its lanterns never go out, and its lighthouse keeper has one job: looking after letters that are not ready to be read yet.

  1. Write

    Sit down at the lighthouse and write to the person you are about to become. Say what you hope for, what worries you, and what today feels like.

  2. Seal

    Choose the day your letter should be opened. It is folded, corked into a bottle and set afloat. From that moment it stays closed until its day.

  3. Return

    When the day comes, the bottle drifts back to shore. Open it, and meet the person you used to be.

Some letters travel for a few weeks. Others cross years.

The Lighthouse

The writing desk sits at the top of the lighthouse, where the light is warm and nobody is watching.

Here you will be able to:

  • give your letter a title
  • write as much or as little as you like
  • choose the day it will be opened
  • read it through once more before it is sealed
  • seal it and send it out to sea

The writing desk is still being built. Check back soon.

The Ocean

Beyond the pier the water is full of bottles. Each one carries a promise from someone to the person they are about to become.

Every letter drifts at its own pace, sealed against the tide until its day arrives. Some will be read in a month, some in a decade.

One day, a few anonymous messages may wash ashore for everyone to read. Nothing like that will appear without careful thought about privacy and moderation first.

About

NAMI began with a simple question: what would you say to yourself a year from now?

Most of us never ask. Days blur together, and the person we were last spring becomes a stranger. A letter is a small act of care across time, and NAMI gives it a quiet place to wait.

NAMI is an independent project, built by hand with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP and MySQL, and a lasting fondness for old pixel-art games.

Questions

Can I open a letter early?

No. A sealed letter stays sealed until the date you chose.

Who can read my letters?

Letters are private to the person who wrote them.

Will I be told when my bottle arrives?

Yes. You will receive an email when your letter is ready to be opened.
